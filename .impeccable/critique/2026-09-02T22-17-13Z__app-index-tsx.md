## Design Specificity Verdict
Copy is Grana.-specific; composition is category-interchangeable. RevealOnScroll (fade+translateY16px/600ms) repeats 25+ times identically. Detector corroborates: 7x kicker-above-heading, 29 anti-patterns total live on page. Benchmarked against framer.com, linear.app, vercel.com: Framer uses real scroll-linked parallax + "live" product states (page has one static float loop, not scroll-linked); Linear spreads hover/glow through the whole body (page has hover on only 3 elements total); Vercel varies section *form* by content type (page forces icon+title+paragraph on 4 different content domains); none of the three force 100vh scroll-snap sections (page does, producing slide-deck cadence). Detector also flagged ai-color-palette (4x) and radial-spotlight-glow (2x) — ironic given these are recognized AI-generic-design tells; the CTA/FogBackground glow may be reinforcing the "amateur" feel rather than fixing it.
